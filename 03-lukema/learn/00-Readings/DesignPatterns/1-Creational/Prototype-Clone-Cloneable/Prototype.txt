
Prototype: Make a clone to avoid new creation.

   1. It must implement Cloneable to avoid CloneNotSupportedException and
      to make a field-for-field copy of instances of that class.

   2. It should override Object.clone().

   3. The constructor/setup should be private.

   4. It has a propotype instance to keep a copy of its expensive resources.
      The new cloned object do a member by member copy of the resources.
