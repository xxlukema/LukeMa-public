Thread Deadlock

 

 

Time 0:

  1. Thread1: Gets A.lock. Sleeps for 1 sec to allow Thread2 to get B.lock
  2. Thread2: Gets B.lock. Sleeps for 1 sec to allow Thread1 to get A.lock.

Time 1 sec:

  1. Thread1: Holds A.lock and tries to get B.lock.
  2. Thread2: Holds B.lock and tries to get A.lock.

Time 2 sec:

  1. Deadlock.
