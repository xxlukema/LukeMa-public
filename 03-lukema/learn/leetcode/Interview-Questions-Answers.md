# Interview Questions And Answers

## Where will you be in five years?

[How I Aced Microsoft Interview]<https://www.piratekingdom.com/blog/how-i-aced-microsoft-interview>

"I do not have the slightest idea where I will be in five years, but if there is a place to help me become a better, greater person, I will be there." — PIRATE KING

If there is a place such that the future me, five years later, looks back to the past me. And if that future me can say, 'Boy, have I learned a lot since then. Man,
have I become a better, a greater person since then!' If there is such a place that I can say that, I will be there.
